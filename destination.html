FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about studying abroad, visas or travelling? Here are answers to some of the questions our clients ask most often.

Does Pedani Migration Services guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant embassy or immigration authority. We cannot guarantee approval, but we can help you prepare your application correctly and guide you through the process.

Can I apply for a visa without previous travel experience?

Yes. Having previous travel history is not always a requirement. What matters is presenting a genuine application with the appropriate documents and meeting the requirements for your chosen visa.

Can you help me with admission to study abroad?

Yes. We assist prospective students with finding suitable study options, understanding admission requirements and navigating the application process for eligible institutions.

Do I need a sponsor for my study or visa application?

This depends on your destination, visa category and individual circumstances. Where financial sponsorship is required, we can explain the relevant requirements and documentation.

What happens if my visa application is refused?

A refusal does not necessarily mean you cannot apply again. We can help you understand the reason for the decision and discuss the appropriate next steps for a future application.

How long does visa processing take?

Processing times vary depending on the destination, visa category, application volume and the relevant immigration authority. We will provide guidance based on the requirements applicable to your case.

Can you help with flights and accommodation?

Yes. We can assist with travel arrangements such as flight bookings and accommodation to help make your journey more organized and convenient.

Can I apply if I don't live in Lagos?

Yes. You can contact our team remotely to discuss your requirements. We'll let you know which parts of the process can be completed online and when physical attendance may be necessary.

What documents will I need for my application?

Required documents depend on the type of application and destination. After reviewing your situation, we'll provide guidance on the documents relevant to your application.

How do I get started with Pedani Migration Services?

Simply reach out to our team and tell us what you're planning — whether it's studying abroad, applying for a visa or travelling. We'll help you understand your options and the next steps.
