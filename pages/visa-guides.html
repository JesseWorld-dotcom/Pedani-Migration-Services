Before You Apply

Start With the Right Information

A successful visa application begins with understanding the purpose of your trip, selecting the appropriate visa category and preparing the required supporting documents.

Visa rules differ from one country to another, and requirements can also depend on your personal circumstances, travel purpose and intended length of stay.

What should you check?

  • The purpose and duration of your trip
  • The visa category that matches your plans
  • Passport validity and travel history
  • Financial and supporting documentation
  • Application forms and appointment requirements
  • Current processing and submission instructions

Why preparation matters

Missing information or inconsistent documents can create unnecessary delays. Preparing early gives you time to identify gaps and make sure your application is presented clearly.
